01 — Logo & Mark

The Sprout Mark

A single continuous stroke does three jobs at once: it reads as a lowercase b, it curls like a fern frond unfurling, and it closes into a focus ring around a bud. One idea, not three icons stacked together — that restraint is the point.

Primary mark — monoline, single weight, no gradients

Built on a 100×100 grid from one 9pt stroke: a vertical stem (the letterform) and a spiral curl (the growth motion) meeting at a solid bud — the "next rep," the reason to open the app today.

App icon — Android adaptive squircle, 3 surface variants

Foreground mark sits on a safe zone at 66% of the icon canvas so it survives circle, squircle and rounded-square system masks without clipping the bud.

Clear space & minimum size

Minimum clear space equals the width of the stem stroke on every side. Never render the mark smaller than 20px (app icon) or 16px (favicon) — the bud disappears first.

What to avoid

Don't rotate or tilt

Don't add gradients

Don't thin the stroke

03 — Typography

Rounded, geometric, legible at 12px

A two-typeface system plus a mono for numbers — because a productivity app lives or dies on how it displays a timer, an XP count, and a streak number.

Display & headings

Plus Jakarta Sans

Geometric, rounded terminals, confident at large sizes — carries the brand's warmth in headlines, onboarding, and the wordmark.

600 SemiBold700 Bold800 ExtraBold — hero moments only

Body & UI

Inter

Neutral, dense, engineered for small UI text — labels, body copy, buttons, and settings where clarity matters more than personality.

400 Regular500 Medium600 SemiBold — labels/buttons

Numeric & data

JetBrains Mono

Tabular figures for timers, XP counters, and streak numbers — so digits don't jitter sideways as a session counts down.

500 Medium600 SemiBold — large counters

Pairing rationale

Jakarta's roundness echoes the logo's curl; Inter's neutrality keeps 40 screens of study content easy to scan; the mono face gives progress numbers structure without shouting. All three share the same x-height rhythm at UI sizes.
